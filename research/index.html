OPEN SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH

Research without barriers.

Tardonaut supports the exploration of biological systems, medical science and environmental restoration.

Our objective is to publish accessible research materials, documented datasets and scientific findings.

Explore research
RESEARCH AREAS

Our research interests

Six initial categories organize our planned research activities.

01

Medical Biotechnology

Exploring biological processes and their potential applications in medical science.

02

Tardigrade Biology

Investigating the remarkable survival mechanisms of tardigrades.

03

DNA Protection

Studying DNA stability, damage, repair and potential protective mechanisms.

04

Biological Preservation

Exploring methods for preserving biological materials and systems.

05

Environmental Biotechnology

Investigating biological approaches to environmental challenges.

06

Water Research

Exploring water quality, biodegradation and ecosystem restoration.
